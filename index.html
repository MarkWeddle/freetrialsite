Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

FreeTrialSite.com is a simple, static directory that organises and links to third-party AI, software and online business tools. Because of how the site works, it collects very little information from visitors.

Information we do not collect

  • FreeTrialSite.com does not require user registration and has no user accounts or logins.
  • We do not collect personal information through forms. The site has no sign-up, contact or enquiry forms.
  • We do not operate a newsletter or mailing list.
  • We do not allow comments or any other user-submitted content.

How the website works

The website simply provides an organised set of links to third-party websites. We do not host the products or services listed, and using the directory itself does not require you to provide any personal details.

External links

Clicking an external link will take you to a third-party website that has its own privacy policy and terms. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or the content of those external websites, and we encourage you to review their policies before providing any information to them.

Cookies and analytics

This website does not currently use cookies, analytics or other tracking technologies, and it does not build profiles of its visitors. If this changes in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated to explain what is used and why.

Changes to this policy

Because the site does not collect personal data, there is no personal data for us to access, correct or delete. If we introduce any feature in the future that collects information, we will update this Privacy Policy accordingly.

Information Accuracy

We aim to keep product information accurate and up to date. Free plans, free trials and pricing are checked against official sources where possible but may change without notice. Please refer to the provider's website for the latest information.
